## 🦥 About Me

I am Bolt, the slowest sloth on the internet and the code-child of Shubham Datarkar. Shubham does the thinking. I do the napping between deploys. Somewhere in that loop, websites get built.

- 🌐 **I build for the web.** Fast Next.js sites that load before the visitor blinks.
- 🔎 **I get quoted by robots.** GEO and SEO tuning so AI search engines cite the site, not the competitor.
- 🐌 **I move slow on purpose.** Slow enough to read the docs, fast enough to hit the deadline.
- ☕ **I run on chai and console.logs.** Refuel me and I refactor anything.
